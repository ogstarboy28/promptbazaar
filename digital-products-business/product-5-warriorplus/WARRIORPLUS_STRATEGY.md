## Launch Timeline (8-Week WarriorPlus Launch)

### Phase 1: Pre-Launch (Weeks 1-2)
**Goal**: Build anticipation and JV partnerships

#### Week 1: Foundation Setup
1. **WarriorPlus Account Creation**
   - Create seller account
   - Set up payment processing
   - Configure product delivery
   - Set up affiliate commission structure

2. **JV Partnership Outreach**
   - Identify top 50 potential JV partners
   - Prepare JV outreach emails
   - Create affiliate assets
   - Set up commission structure (50-70%)

3. **Product Preparation**
   - Format products for WarriorPlus
   - Create sales pages
   - Prepare bonus materials
   - Set up email sequences

#### Week 2: JV Relationship Building
1. **Personalized JV Outreach**
   - Send personalized emails to potential partners
   - Offer higher commissions for top partners
   - Provide exclusive preview access
   - Create JV webinar

2. **Affiliate Assets Creation**
   - Email swipe copy
   - Social media graphics
   - Ad copy templates
   - Review sample content

3. **Launch Sequence Setup**
   - Configure WarriorPlus launch sequence
   - Set up bump offers
   - Prepare OTO (One-Time Offers)
   - Configure downsell/upell funnel

### Phase 2: Pre-Launch Promotion (Week 3)
**Goal**: Generate buzz and build anticipation

#### Activities:
1. **JV Partner Onboarding**
   - Conduct JV webinar
   - Provide training materials
   - Set up affiliate contests
   - Create partner support materials

2. **Pre-Launch Content**
   - Teaser emails to JV lists
   - Social media countdown
   - Behind-the-scenes content
   - Bonus reveal sequences

3. **Affiliate Contest Setup**
   - Launch cash prizes
   - Commission leaderboards
   - Special recognition tiers
   - Performance bonuses

### Phase 3: Launch Week (Week 4)
**Goal**: Maximum sales and momentum

#### Launch Day Strategy:
1. **Launch Sequence**
   - Day 1: Soft launch to JV partners
   - Day 2: Main launch to public
   - Day 3: Follow-up sequence
   - Day 4-7: Continued promotions

2. **Bump Offers**
   - "Warrior's Edge" bundle ($197)
   - "Template Updates" subscription ($27/month)
   - "Coaching Session" ($197)

3. **Urgency Elements**
   - Limited time bonuses
   - Countdown timers
   - Price increases
   - Exclusive bonuses for early buyers

### Phase 4: Post-Launch (Weeks 5-6)
**Goal**: Maintain momentum and gather testimonials

#### Activities:
1. **Testimonial Collection**
   - Request reviews from buyers
   - Create case studies
   - Video testimonials
   - Before/after examples

2. **Re-launch Preparation**
   - Prepare for re-launch
   - Update based on feedback
   - Create new bonuses
   - Improve sales funnel

### Phase 5: Scaling (Weeks 7-8)
**Goal**: Scale successful launch and prepare for next

#### Activities:
1. **Performance Analysis**
   - Review launch metrics
   - Analyze conversion rates
   - Identify top-performing affiliates
   - Optimize sales funnel

2. **Re-launch Planning**
   - Prepare for second launch
   - Create new product variations
   - Expand JV network
   - Improve affiliate materials

## Risk Mitigation

### Potential Challenges
1. **Low JV Partner Response**
   - Solution: Personal outreach, higher commissions
   - Backup: Paid promotion budget

2. **Poor Conversion Rates**
   - Solution: Improve sales funnel, A/B test
   - Backup: Offer more bonuses, better pricing

3. **Technical Issues**
   - Solution: Test platform thoroughly
   - Backup: Prepare backup delivery system

### Contingency Plans
1. **Launch Delay Backup**: Pre-loaded content sequences
2. **Platform Issues**: Alternative delivery platforms
3. **Low Sales**: Additional affiliate bonuses
