# Digital Products Business Project Plan

## Overview
Building 5 digital products to solve worldwide business problems with zero investment setup.

## Timeline
- **Month 1**: Research, setup, and Product 1 launch
- **Month 2**: Products 2-4 launch and marketing
- **Month 3**: Product 5 launch and full momentum

## Products to Build

### Product 1: Productivity Mastery Bootcamp
**Problem**: Remote workers struggling with time management
**Solution**: Online course with videos, templates, and action plans
**Launch**: Week 1
**Platform**: Teachable/Podia (free setup)

### Product 2: Small Business Marketing Templates Bundle  
**Problem**: Small businesses don't know how to create marketing materials
**Solution**: Canva templates + social media content + email campaigns
**Launch**: Week 2
**Platform**: Etsy/Gumroad (free setup)

### Product 3: Business Automation Playbook
**Problem**: Business owners waste time on repetitive tasks
**Solution**: Step-by-step guide + automation tools + workflow templates
**Launch**: Week 3
**Platform**: PDF/eBook format

### Product 4: Digital Skills Accelerator
**Problem**: People need to learn digital skills quickly
**Solution**: Mini-courses on AI, social media, digital tools
**Launch**: Week 4
**Platform**: Video courses with free hosting

### Product 5: Entrepreneur's Success Toolkit
**Problem**: New entrepreneurs need all-in-one solutions
**Solution**: Bundle of all previous products + exclusive content
**Launch**: Week 5
**Platform**: Membership site or upgraded bundle

## Revenue Goals
- **Month 1**: $500-$1,000 (first sales)
- **Month 2**: $2,000-$3,000 (more products launched)
- **Month 3**: $5,000+ (full momentum)
