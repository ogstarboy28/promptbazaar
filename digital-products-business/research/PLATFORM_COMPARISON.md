# Course Platform Comparison: Teachable vs Podia

## Executive Summary
For our digital products business starting with zero investment, **Teachable's free plan is the best choice** for Product 1. It allows us to start immediately without cost and has room to grow.

## Platform Analysis

### Teachable
**Free Plan Features:**
- ✅ 2 courses (perfect for our first product)
- ✅ Up to 100 students (great for initial testing)
- ✅ Course certificates
- ✅ Basic sales pages
- ✅ Affiliate program access
- ✅ Mobile app support
- ✅ Student progress tracking

**Pricing for Growth:**
- Starter: $39/month (7.5% transaction fee) - 1 product, 100 students
- Builder: $89/month (0% fee) - 5 products, 1,000 students
- Growth: $189/month (0% fee) - 25 products, 5,000 students

**Pros:**
- Best free plan available
- Excellent course creation tools
- Strong student management
- Good mobile experience
- Established platform with proven track record

**Cons:**
- Limited products on free plan (2 courses)
- Transaction fees on lower tiers
- Limited website customization
- No built-in email marketing
- No blogging feature

### Podia
**Trial/Freemium:**
- ✅ 30-day free trial
- Mover: $39/month (5% transaction fee) - unlimited products
- Shaker: $89/month (0% fee) - unlimited products + affiliate marketing

**Pros:**
- All-in-one platform (courses, downloads, webinars, coaching)
- Unlimited products even on entry plans
- Built-in website, blog, and email marketing
- Beautiful, modern interface
- Free migrations from other platforms

**Cons:**
- No truly free plan (30-day trial only)
- More complex interface
- Higher starting cost if we need multiple features
- Less focused on course-specific features

## Decision: Teachable Free Plan

### Why Teachable is Better for Our Starting Point:
1. **Zero Investment**: Start completely free with 2 courses and 100 students
2. **Course-Focused**: Better course creation and management tools
3. **Scalable Path**: Clear upgrade path as we grow
4. **Proven Platform**: Established with successful course creators
5. **Simple Setup**: Easy to get started quickly

### Upgrade Strategy:
- **Phase 1**: Use free plan for initial launch (Months 1-2)
- **Phase 2**: Upgrade to Starter plan ($39/month) when we exceed 100 students or need more courses
- **Phase 3**: Upgrade to Builder plan ($89/month) when we hit 5 products or 1,000 students

## Action Plan

### Week 1: Teachable Setup
1. **Create Teachable Account**: Sign up for free plan
2. **School Branding**: Set up school name, logo, colors
3. **Course Structure**: Create course outline in Teachable
4. **Basic Pages**: Set up landing page and about page

### Week 2: Content Upload
1. **Video Upload**: Record and upload course videos
2. **PDF Resources**: Upload worksheets and templates
3. **Quizzes**: Create knowledge check quizzes
4. **Certificates**: Set up completion certificates

### Week 3: Marketing Integration
1. **Sales Page**: Optimize for conversions
2. **Email Integration**: Connect with email service
3. **Affiliate Setup**: Prepare affiliate program
4. **Payment Plans**: Set up payment options

### Week 4: Launch Preparation
1. **Testing**: Test all student experience
2. **Final Review**: Review course content
3. **Launch Materials**: Prepare marketing copy
4. **Community**: Set up student discussion area

## Future Considerations

### When to Switch to Podia:
- If we need unlimited products before hitting revenue goals
- If we want built-in email marketing and blog
- If we plan to sell multiple product types (courses, downloads, coaching)
- If we need more website customization

### Alternative Strategy:
- Keep Teachable for courses
- Use Podia for other digital products (templates, downloads)
- Use separate platforms for each product type based on strengths

## Budget Timeline
- **Months 1-2**: $0 (Teachable free plan)
- **Months 3-4**: $39/month (Teachable Starter)
- **Months 5+**: $89/month (Teachable Builder)
- **Total Year 1**: ~$1,000 in platform costs

This gives us a solid foundation to start generating revenue before investing in more expensive platforms.
